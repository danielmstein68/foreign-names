DECISION: - **2026-10-24 · regress:** Local candidates with missing incumbency (18%) are kept, with incumbent set to 0 and a missing-incumbency indicator added. *Why:* Dropping them would lose many contests; the indicator stops the fill-in from biasing the incumbency coefficient.
DECISION: - **2026-10-24 · regress:** For local partisan races, the party control is HKS's estimated party (pid_est: D/R/unknown), because HKS does not record the ballot party. *Why:* It is the only party information available for local races; it is an estimate, not the label voters saw.
DECISION: - **2026-10-24 · regress:** Overperformance baseline is the party's share of the presidential vote (of all votes cast) in the most recent presidential election in or before the race's year: state level for Senate, county level for local partisan (2000+, 5-digit county FIPS only). Only Democrats and Republicans enter this design. *Why:* Matches the candidate vote-share outcome, which is also a share of all votes.
DECISION: - **2026-10-24 · regress:** Swing design: House and Senate races with exactly one scored Democrat and one scored Republican; Democratic two-party share on (Democrat score − Republican score), with district × redistricting-cycle fixed effects (state for Senate), year fixed effects, and both incumbency flags. Not run for local partisan races. *Why:* District-cycle effects make each district its own baseline. Local districts can't be followed reliably over time in HKS.
DECISION: - **2026-10-24 · regress:** Pooled models allow party effects to differ by source (party × source dummies). *Why:* The contest fixed effect already absorbs source.
